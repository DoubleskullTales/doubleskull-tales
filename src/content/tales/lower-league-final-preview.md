After the group stage, the Paradice League splits in two. The upper half plays for the championship. The lower half plays for something every coach in the league understands just as well: not going home empty-handed.

That Lower League has its final too, and it's on the same night as the big one. On Tuesday, December 1, at 18:00 at Jo&Jo by Westbahnhof, Coach Ordog's Scorpion Kings meet Coach Jear's Nuffle's Lil' Helperz. Dust against dirt. Ancient kings against rats in the sewer. And one coach who has waited three seasons for another final against one who got there in his first.

The hitting is done by the Tomb Guardians, four walking statues with Brawler and Decay who simply don't fall over. One of them alone has six casualties. The scoring is done by the Throwers, which for Tomb Kings is about as unusual as it sounds. And FelinoFury's Dwarfs can tell you all about it: they lost to the Scorpion Kings twice this season, 2:0 in the group and 1:0 in the semi-final.

For Ordog, this final has a history. He has been in the league since Season 2, and in that very season he reached the final and lost it to Tok Janne. Three seasons later, he's back in one.

## Nuffle's Lil' Helperz

**Coach Jear · Ferenzy Division**

Read the roster and you'll notice something. The Troll is called Santa. The rats are Comet, Blitzen, Rudolph, Dasher, Dancer and Prancer. The Goblins answer to Alabaster Snowball, Bushy Evergreen, Shinny Upatree, Wunorse Openslae and Pepper Minstix. Nuffle's little helpers, indeed. And a final on December 1, the day the first Advent calendar door opens, feels like it was planned.

It hasn't all been Christmas, though. Comet, Blitzen, Pepper Minstix, Sparkle and Jingle are all on their second edition. And Jear's first season in the Paradice League started rough: three wins and four losses in the group stage, with 22 casualties suffered and only 6 caused.

Then the playoffs came, and the Helperz flipped a switch. Three games, three wins, seven touchdowns. They still got beaten up along the way. They just didn't care.

## Common enemies

Three opponents played both finalists this season. The results tell you a lot about the two teams.

- **Dante's Da Green Bay Whackers:** 1:1 against the Scorpion Kings in the group. 3:1 for the Helperz in the round of 16.
- **Discord's Black Rage:** 1:0 for the Scorpion Kings in the group. 2:1 for the Helperz in the semi-final.
- **Borgor's Black Orcs:** a forfeit win for the Scorpion Kings in the round of 16. A 2:1 win for the Helperz in the group.

Same opponents, different style. The Scorpion Kings grind them down. The Helperz run past them.

## The Daily Skull's verdict

On paper it's close. The Scorpion Kings have the higher team value, the better group record and a defence that hasn't conceded a touchdown in the playoffs. The Helperz have Comet II, who has scored twice as many touchdowns on his own as the entire Scorpion Kings team.

That's the whole final in one question: can four slow Tomb Guardians catch a rat with MA 9, Dodge, Sidestep and a spare head? If they can, Ordog wins 1:0, as usual. If they can't, it's going to be a long evening for the kings.

The lower team value also means Jear can spend 170,000 gold on inducements, and his treasury is far from empty. A slight edge to the Helperz, then. But only slight.

## The subplot

Caliritus, the Scorpion Kings' best scorer, has Hatred (Human). There isn't a single human on the other side. Not one. Somebody please tell him before kick-off.

And then there's Santa. The Troll hasn't earned a single SPP all season. On December 1, of all days, that could change.
