In the Paradice League, old players don't retire. They send an invoice. And not every mercenary works for everyone: each one only signs for certain teams. So before the Season 5 final on Tuesday, December 1, we looked at the list and asked a simple question: who could the finalists bring along?

We start with Coach Xurpils' Back to the Roots. Five mercenaries are willing to play for the Wood Elves. Two of them are elves, two are gnomes, and one is a tree with a very long memory.

One thing to keep in mind: Back to the Roots go into the final with the higher team value, so any mercenary has to come out of Xurpils' own treasury. Luckily, the treasury is well stocked, and the whole league expects him to open it. If not for a final, then when? The only question is who he spends it on. Which is exactly what we're asking you at the end.

## Vorith: the roll you never saw coming

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-vorith.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Vorith, Wardancer, ex Sylvaneth Woodland Raiders (Coach Alexej): MA 8, ST 3, AG 2+, PA 4+, AV 8+. Dodge, Jump Up, Leap, Sneaky Git, Block, Loner (4+). 2025: 2 TD, 1 CAS, 1 MVP. Hire fee 220k." />
</div>

Vorith learned his trade with Coach Alexej's Sylvaneth Woodland Raiders. In 2025 he scored 2 touchdowns, caused a casualty and picked up an MVP. The numbers don't tell the whole story, though.

Vorith is a master of two arts. The first is getting back up: knock him down and he rolls away and is on his feet before you've finished celebrating. The second is the kind of foul only a few referees ever spot. Sneaky Git isn't on his card by accident.

He's the most expensive option on the list. He's also the one opposing coaches like least.

## Celebrimbor: fast, and not very polite

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-celebrimbor.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Celebrimbor, Wood Elf Catcher, ex Woodshelfs (Coach Jack1994): MA 8, ST 2, AG 2+, PA 3+, AV 8+. Catch, Dodge, Sprint, Dirty Player, Block, Loner (4+). 2025: 1 TD, 2 MVP. Hire fee 190k." />
</div>

Trained by Coach Jack1994 for the Woodshelfs, Celebrimbor looks like a classic Wood Elf Catcher: quick, light, good hands. 1 touchdown and 2 MVPs in 2025.

Then you read the rest of the card. Dirty Player. Block. Celebrimbor isn't just fast. He's very, very hard when he puts the boot in. Most catchers try to stay away from the rough stuff. This one goes looking for it.

## Maccherone: the tree that remembers

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-maccherone.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Maccherone, Treeman, ex Pasta e Basta (Coach Umberto): MA 2, ST 6, AG 5+, PA 5+, AV 11+. Pro, Grab, Mighty Blow, Stand Firm, Strong Arm, Thick Skull, Loner (4+), Take Root, Timmm-ber!, Throw Team-mate. 2025: 4 CAS, 3 completions, 3 MVP. Hire fee 190k." />

Here's where it gets interesting. Maccherone spent the 2025 season with Pasta e Basta, a Halfling team. The coach? Umberto. The same Umberto who will be standing on the other side of the pitch on December 1.

Maccherone was no passenger in that team either: 4 casualties, 3 completed passes and 3 MVPs. He knows Umberto's plays, his habits, his favourite tricks. And as everyone knows, trees have very long memories.

Would Xurpils hire his opponent's former player to beat him? It worked for Umberto in the semi-final against Smaug. Turnabout would be fair play.

## Makwess: as fast as a fox

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-makwess.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Makwess, Gnome Beastmaster, ex My little farm is running wild (Coach Shizo): MA 5, ST 2, AG 3+, PA 4+, AV 8+. Dodge, Jump Up, Sprint, Wrestle, Guard, Loner (4+), Stunty. 2025: 3 MVP. Hire fee 105k." />
</div>

Makwess played for Coach Shizo's My little farm is running wild in 2025 and was the fastest gnome on the team. His secret: he rode a goose. On a goose, a gnome is as fast as a fox. Nobody in the league has found a rule against it yet.

3 MVPs in a single season, and at 105k he's the cheapest mercenary on the list. Wrestle and Guard make him far more useful than his size suggests.

## Jibbrugock: the toughest garden gnome

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-jibbrugock.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Jibbrugock, Gnome, ex My little farm is running wild (Coach Shizo): MA 5, ST 2, AG 3+, PA 4+, AV 7+. Dodge, Jump Up, Pro, Wrestle, Loner (4+), Right Stuff, Stunty. 2025: 1 TD, 3 CAS, 2 MVP. Hire fee 120k." />

Same team, same coach, very different gnome. Jibbrugock caused one third of all the casualties My little farm is running wild managed in 2025. On a team of gnomes, that makes him the hardest thing in the garden. 1 touchdown and 2 MVPs on top.

Don't let the pointy hat fool you.
