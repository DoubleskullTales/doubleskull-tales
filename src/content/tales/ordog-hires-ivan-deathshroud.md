Umberto has picked his mercenaries for the big final. Now the Lower League final has its first star too. Coach Ordog didn't wait for the weekend: the Scorpion Kings will bring Ivan 'The Animal' Deathshroud to Tuesday's final against Coach Jear's Nuffle's Lil' Helperz.

It's not a league mercenary this time. Ivan is a proper Star Player with a reputation that's older than most kingdoms. So before he takes the field at Jo&Jo, here's who the kings have just hired.

## Who is Ivan?

According to the official background, Ivan was a warlord long before he was a blitzer. In life he led campaign after campaign against the dwarf holds of the Worlds Edge Mountains, back when the world still settled its arguments with wars instead of touchdowns. The dwarfs, famously, write everything down. They have 1,524 grudges against his name, which is close to a record.

That was about 1,400 years ago. He has been a skeleton ever since.

His most recent comeback was the work of a necromantic coach who wanted more punch in his lineup. Ivan woke up, looked around and hated what he saw: a world without war. Then he watched his first game of this sport and changed his mind. Hitting people in front of a paying crowd turned out to be close enough.

He had a successful spell with the Hel Fenn Hellions, then went looking for the treasure he'd lost in his warlord days. He found enough gold to buy out his own contract. Since then, he's been a free agent, moving from one undead team to the next, preferably to the ones with dwarfs on their fixture list. A chance meeting with the Grudge Bearers blew his cover, and now fans follow him around the league hoping he'll settle their ancestors' old scores.

## The small problem

Look at the card again. Hatred (Dwarf). Dwarven Scourge, a special rule that gets him through armour once per game, and even better against dwarfs.

Now look at the opposition. The Helperz are rats and goblins. There isn't a dwarf among them. Not one.

Regular readers will know this feeling. Caliritus, the Scorpion Kings' top scorer, has Hatred (Human), and there are no humans in Jear's team either. That's two Scorpion Kings with a grudge and nobody to take it out on. Ivan will have to settle for rats. He'll cope.

## What he does bring

Strip the dwarf rules away and Ivan is still a very good blitzer, and he fits this final well.

- **Tackle** takes Dodge away. Comet II, the Gutter Runner with 14 of the Helperz' 15 touchdowns, relies on Dodge and Sidestep to get out of trouble. Against Ivan, Dodge doesn't help him.
- **Strip Ball** means that if Ivan pushes the ball carrier back, the ball goes on the ground. Comet II has no Sure Hands. Rudolph, the thrower, does, so Ivan will need to pick his target.
- **Disturbing Presence** makes passing and catching harder for anyone nearby. Rudolph has 14 completions this season, and he won't enjoy having 1,400 years of bad temper standing a few squares away.
- **Block, Juggernaut and Regeneration** do the rest. He hits reliably on the blitz, and if somebody does manage to injure him, there's a decent chance he simply pulls himself back together. On a Tomb Kings roster, that's practically team tradition.

The plan is easy to read. Ordog's slow kings have one question to answer on Tuesday: can they catch the rat? Ordog has just hired the best rat-catcher he could afford.

## A note for the rules historians

Ivan isn't new. He first appeared in the 2016 edition of the game, where his rules were largely a rework of an old Khemri star called Setekh. So in a roundabout way, a star with Khemri roots is back in the desert. Whether Ivan knows this is unclear.

His fee has moved too. When his miniature came out in 2023, he cost 190,000 gold. Today, Ordog pays 210,000. Inflation, it seems, reaches even the afterlife.

## Your move, Jear

The Helperz haven't announced their plans yet. Coach Jear will decide over the weekend, and he has already said he'll spend everything he has. So almost anything is possible. Santa may be bringing a few extra helpers.

Until then, the prediction game for the Lower League final is still open. Does Ivan change your call?
