Umberto made his choice this afternoon. Xurpils hasn't announced anything. Instead, a picture landed in your correspondent's inbox. It showed Deeproot Strongbranch.

Nothing is confirmed. But in the Paradice League, a coach doesn't send you a picture of a tree by accident.

## Not one of ours

Everybody on Xurpils' original shortlist learned the game in this league. Deeproot did not. He is a Star Player in the old sense of the word: the most famous treeman the game has ever seen, and he was around long before any of us rolled a block die.

According to the old record books, he was the first treeman ever to play. He started his career with a halfling team, the Greenfield Grasshuggers, in 2423. Eleven years later the Grasshuggers scored two touchdowns without the ball touching the ground, thanks to halflings he had thrown downfield. Asked by a reporter what annoyed him most, he named woodpeckers.

Today he signs for Woodland teams: halflings, gnomes and wood elves. So Back to the Roots are allowed to call.

**Special rule, Reliable:** if he fumbles a Throw Team-mate, the thrown player bounces as usual but always lands safely.

Strength 7 with Block. Against any ST3 player on Umberto's side, that's three dice. Against the ST4 hitters, including both of Umberto's new signings, it's still two. Add Stand Firm, Mighty Blow, Armour 11+ and Thick Skull, and you get something that is very hard to move and very unpleasant to stand next to.

The catch is Movement 2. Deeproot doesn't go anywhere quickly. Wood Elves usually do.

## The halfling problem

His most famous trick is throwing small people. Throw Team-mate, Strong Arm, Bullseye: everything is there. There's just one issue. Wood Elves don't have anyone with Right Stuff, so there's nobody on the roster he can throw.

Unless Xurpils brings his own ammunition. Jibbrugock the gnome was on his original shortlist, and Jibbrugock has Right Stuff. We're not saying. We're just saying.

## Why borrow a tree when you can rent the original?

There was already a treeman on Xurpils' list: Maccherone, trained by Umberto himself for Pasta e Basta. 4 casualties, 3 completions and 3 MVPs in 2025, and nobody knows Umberto's tactics better. We even asked on Instagram whether Xurpils should hire Umberto's old tree.

Maybe that question was too easy for him. Deeproot hits harder than Maccherone and has Block on top. And unlike Maccherone, he doesn't have Take Root, so he won't suddenly decide the pitch is a nice place to grow.

What Deeproot doesn't have is inside information. Maccherone has spent a whole season in Umberto's dugout. The original has never met Umberto. He probably doesn't need to.

## Rumour or reality?

We'll know on Tuesday at the latest, when Back to the Roots meet Pop Khorne at Jo&Jo am Westbahnhof. Kick-off is 18:00.

Until then, the fan vote for Xurpils is still open. Deeproot isn't on that list, because he isn't a league mercenary. If you think Xurpils should go for the original anyway, tell him at the bar. If you'd rather see Umberto's old tree, or two of the other four, vote here.
