One thing is already certain before the first kick-off: the Paradice League will crown a new champion. Lewdgrip's Orcs, Tok Janne's Ogres, Bachmut's Humans and Outcaarst's Skaven. Every former title holder is out. On December 1, two teams fight for a trophy that has never seen their name.

## Pop Khorne

**Coach Umberto · Bloodweiser Division**

Pop Khorne opened the season with a 0:1 loss to Black Orcs. They haven't lost since. Two 2:1 wins against Gnomes and Skaven followed, a 2:0 against Amazons and two hard-fought draws against the Old World Alliance and Orcs.

It wasn't free. Somewhere along the way, a Treeman put the team's Bloodspawn into the ground for good. Pop Khorne kept marching anyway.

In the playoffs they beat Coach Alexej's Lustria Harpies 2:1, knocked out reigning champion Outcaarst and his Nurgle side Seuchenkontrolle 1:0, and shut down Coach Smaug's Morathis Murders 2:0 in the semi-final.

### Player to watch: Pop Seeker Master

A Bloodseeker with Block, Guard and Stand Firm. One touchdown, five casualties. You don't move him. He moves you.

## Back to the Roots

**Coach Xurpils · Dead Bull Division**

The Wood Elves went 5 wins, 1 draw and a single loss (to Bretonnians) through the group stage. Then they turned it up. Goblins 5:0, Skaven 2:1 and Coach Spitsens' Goff Raidaz 2:0 in the semi-final. Nine touchdowns scored in the playoffs, one conceded.

### Player to watch: Khiacas

A Wardancer with AG1+, Strip Ball and Side Step. Eight touchdowns, four casualties, one completion and two MVPs this season. Good luck catching him.

## The subplot

Pop Khorne already lost their Bloodspawn to a Treeman this season. Now they face the tree huggers themselves. Grudges have been settled for less.

> Blood or roots? The Daily Skull will be pitchside on December 1.
