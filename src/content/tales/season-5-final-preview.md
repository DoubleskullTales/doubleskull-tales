One thing is already certain before the first kick-off: the Paradice League will crown a new champion. Lewdgrip, Tok Janne, Bachmut, Outcaarst. Every coach who has ever lifted the trophy is out. And both finalists had a hand in it.

On Tuesday, December 1, at 18:00, Coach Umberto's Pop Khorne meet Coach Xurpils' Back to the Roots. Blood against roots. Frenzy against footwork. And two coaches who know exactly what they're doing: Umberto is one of the best Khorne coaches around, Xurpils one of the best Wood Elf coaches. Both also play alongside your correspondent in the Austrian national team, so this one is personal for everyone.

## The fallen champions

Four former champions entered Season 5. None of them made the final, and both finalists left their mark on the list.

- **Lewdgrip (Season 1):** your correspondent went out with his Orcs in an Orc mirror match against Spitsens' Goffz Raidaz. They were then knocked out by Xurpils' Wood Elves in the semi-final.
- **Tok Janne (Season 2):** his Gnomes, You're Out-Gnome-Bered!, lost 1:2 to Pop Khorne in the group stage.
- **Bachmut (Season 3):** his Goblins, The Gobstoppers, were run over 5:0 by Back to the Roots in the playoffs.
- **Outcaarst (Season 4):** the reigning champion held Back to the Roots to a 1:1 draw in the group stage, then went out 0:1 against Pop Khorne in the playoffs.

## The Daily Skull's verdict

Xurpils goes in as the clear favourite. Wood Elves are simply the better team at scoring, and Back to the Roots have proven it all season: Silmef and Khiacas alone have 18 touchdowns between them.

But Pop Khorne have a path. If Umberto manages to thin out the Elves early, the pitch gets wide, the Elves get few and things can turn dark for Back to the Roots very quickly. Every Wood Elf that leaves the pitch in the first half is one less player to score with in the second.

Both coaches are excellent, and both are teammates of mine. So the verdict comes with a wink: favourite Xurpils, but don't bet the treasury on it.

## The subplot

Pop Khorne already lost their Bloodspawn to a Treeman this season. Now they face a team that brings its own: Cruelelm, a Loren Forest Treeman with Mighty Blow, Stand Firm and Guard.

And there is Dhandeddel, a Wardancer with Hatred (Human). Nine of Pop Khorne's eleven players are human. Grudges have been settled for less.
