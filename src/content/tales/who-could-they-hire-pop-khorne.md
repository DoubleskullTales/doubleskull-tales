Part two of our look at the hiring market before the Season 5 final. Last time it was Xurpils' turn. Now it's Coach Umberto and Pop Khorne, and the list is a lot longer: nine mercenaries are willing to fight for the Blood God's favourite team.

Before we start, a confession. Your correspondent trained five of these nine. Coach Lewdgrip declares a conflict of interest and promises to be completely neutral. Mostly.

## The money

Umberto's situation is the opposite of Xurpils'. Pop Khorne go into the final with the lower team value, so they get 185,000 gold in inducements on the house. Umberto can add a little from his own treasury, and with that, any single mercenary on this list is within reach.

And there's a twist: every gold piece Xurpils spends on mercenaries from his own treasury goes straight into Umberto's inducement pot as well. For two mercenaries, Umberto may need his opponent to go shopping first.

## Old friends

Three of the nine have already worn Pop Khorne colours this season. Umberto knows exactly what he'd be getting.

His fee? 185k. Exactly what Umberto gets for free. Coincidence? The manager says yes.

## From your correspondent's dugout

The conflict of interest, continued. Three more of Lewdgrip's former players are on the market.

## Champions and plague

The last three come from other dugouts, and they bring a title, a smell and a mutation.

### Vuk Flammenfaust: a champion's fist

The only mercenary on the list with a championship. Vuk played for Coach Bachmut's Averheim Owlbears in 2024, and the Owlbears won the title. 3 casualties and an MVP along the way. If Umberto wants someone who knows what lifting the trophy feels like, this is his ogre.

Coach FlinkerBaer's Kaos Kerle produced this one in 2025: 2 touchdowns, 2 casualties, 1 MVP. Somewhere along the way, a mutation took over. From the waist up, Vreamnili now looks like a giant leech. He still plays like a Chosen Blocker, though. He just holds on a little longer than the others.
