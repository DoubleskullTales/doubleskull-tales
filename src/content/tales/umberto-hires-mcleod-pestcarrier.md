Nine mercenaries were on the list. Coach Umberto needed two. The decision is in, and it came without drama:

> "Made my choice and hired these two guys for the final. The same I hired for the semi-final."

So Pop Khorne bring back the duo that already got them here: Connor McLeod and Pestcarrier Of Nurgle. The pair that helped beat Smaug's Morathis Murders 2:0 in the semi-final will be in the dugout again on Tuesday, when Pop Khorne meet Xurpils' Back to the Roots at Jo&Jo am Westbahnhof.

## Connor McLeod: the immortal one

Umberto's reasoning, in his own words:

> "Connor McLeod is immortal. I thought that might come in handy in a Blood Bowl game."

Strictly speaking, Regeneration is not on his card. But he does carry a spare head, and in this sport that's close enough. Trained by Coach Smaug for Don't Loose Your Head in 2024, he left 8 casualties and 2 MVPs behind. Block, Frenzy and Mighty Blow mean he doesn't hit people once if he can hit them twice.

Most Wood Elves wear Armour 8+, so that's a scary thought. The risk is the usual one with Frenzy: he has to follow up after every push, and clever elves know how to drag him out of position. Xurpils will have noticed.

## Pestcarrier Of Nurgle: nobody dodges for free

<div class="starcards single">
<img src="/images/card-merc-pestcarrier.jpg" alt="Star Mercenary card Pestcarrier Of Nurgle, Chosen Blocker, Coach Lewdgrip: MA 5, ST 4, AG 3+, PA 4+, AV 10+. Block, Tackle, Horns, Arm Bar, Loner (4+). 2024: 1 TD, 9 CAS, 1 MVP. Hire fee 195k." />

The second signing comes from your correspondent's old dugout. Pestcarrier caused 9 casualties for the Beelzebubis in 2024, plus a touchdown and an MVP. Coach Lewdgrip declared his conflict of interest in the last article and will now try very hard not to look pleased.

On paper, he is the obvious answer to Wood Elves. Tackle takes away Dodge, so every elf who wants to leave his tackle zone has to roll it the honest way. And if that roll fails, Arm Bar makes the landing hurt a little more. Add Horns on the blitz, and a single horn growing out of his temple to go with it.

## Why the same two?

Never change a winning team is an old rule, and it's not the worst one. Between them, McLeod and Pestcarrier caused 17 casualties in their playing days. One hits hard and keeps hitting. The other makes sure nobody runs away from him. Against a team that lives on speed and agility, Umberto is betting on contact.

## Your move, Xurpils

Pop Khorne have their mercenaries. Back to the Roots haven't announced theirs yet. Five Woodland mercenaries are on Xurpils' list, including a familiar treeman who knows Umberto's tactics better than anyone.
